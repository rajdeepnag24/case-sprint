# Real 2025–26 campus briefs (paraphrased, identifying detail removed)

Use for calibration: what briefs look like, what they hide, and how they map to case types.

1. **Ride-hailing + delivery platform, price parity** (Type 5, Diagnose). Restaurants list higher
   prices on the app than in-store to offset commission. Work it across consumers, merchants,
   couriers, platform, regulators; prioritise; MVP; measurement and governance. Brief asks whether
   to solve it at all. *Hidden:* permission to say "not a priority" if argued.
2. **Healthcare data company, payer–provider contracting** (Type 2, New product). Choose ONE of
   three modules (rate benchmarking on price-transparency data / NLP negotiation co-pilot / what-if
   contract simulator). ≤8 slides, wireframe section required, show synergies with the two not
   chosen. Glossary supplied. *Hidden:* the choice is evaluated; domain knowledge must be learned.
3. **Global payments network, corporate-card data** (Type 6, Monetise). What data products; size
   the market; GTM across regions; one metric; what makes it fast to build and adopt. *Hidden:*
   five questions = five sections handed to you.
4. **Adtech, new product with prototype** (Type 2). Four options (self-serve SMB campaign tool,
   anomaly alerts, AI travel personalisation…). 5–7 pages + working prototype on a public URL,
   any vibe-coding tool, 36–48 h. Six evaluation parameters published; noted they'd seen many
   generic AI ideas. *Hidden:* freshness is a criterion.
5. **Payments company → live-events ticketing inside a music-streaming app** (Type 3, New
   vertical). Use listening data vs an incumbent with ~70% share and disliked fees. ≤6 slides,
   fixed budget, 8-month build, don't disrupt listening, path to a stated 3-year revenue target.
   *Hidden:* a financial model is required.
6. **Same company, games inside a streaming service (~1% engagement, no revenue)** (Type 4,
   Strategic bet). Pick one direction; launch plan to a 3-year target under a fixed budget;
   ≤6 slides; exec summary first; explain why rejected options were rejected.
7. **E-commerce player entering video streaming** (Type 3). MVP, target user, challenges,
   metrics, prioritisation. 3 pages or 5 slides, ≥10 pt font. Every point backed by a known or
   assumed data point. Invited to argue it's a bad idea.
8. **Adtech/mobile marketing, AI problem** (Type 4 with Type 2). How is advertising evolving; what
   high-impact problem can AI solve now? Pick one advertiser + one publisher persona from given
   sets; one problem; say which side you solve for and how the other is affected.
