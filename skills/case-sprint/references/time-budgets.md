# Time budgets by window

Work backwards from the deadline. Always reserve the final buffer for assembly and checks.

| Step | 48 h window | 24 h window | 6 h window |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Brief read + Gate 1 | 30 min | 20 min | 15 min |
| 2 Research pack + Gate 2 | 3 h | 2 h | 45 min |
| 3 Modules + Gate 3a | 3 h | 2 h | 1 h |
| 4 Storyline + Gate 3b | 1.5 h | 1 h | 30 min |
| 5 Deck build | 3 h | 2 h | 1 h 15 min |
| 6 Prototype (optional) | 4 h | 3 h | skip unless required |
| 7 Deck QA + fixes | 1.5 h | 1 h | 45 min |
| 8 Buffer: assembly, links, submit | 2 h | 1 h | 30 min |
| Sleep / coursework | rest | rest | — |

Active hours assumed: 48 h ≈ 18–20 working hours; 24 h ≈ 10–12; 6 h ≈ 5.

## Fallbacks when behind
1. Drop the optional prototype (log it). If required, cut to golden path + labelled demo data.
2. Research: keep precedents + one user-voice source; skip sizing cross-check (state top-down only,
   flag as estimate).
3. Modules: one persona instead of two; three risks instead of five.
4. Appendix: assumptions table only.
Never drop: problem framing, the type's distinctive slide, metrics with targets, the buffer.
